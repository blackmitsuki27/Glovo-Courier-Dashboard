Written from the numbers above
    How each metric is defined
    Delivery time
    Minutes from order created to handed to the customer. Cancelled and in-progress orders are left out.
    On-time rate
    Delivered orders whose delivery time is within the promised minutes recorded on the order.
    Courier utilization
    Minutes a courier holds an accepted order divided by minutes they are online.
    Wait at restaurant
    Minutes between the courier arriving at the pickup point and leaving with the food.
    Waiting for courier
    Orders placed but not yet accepted by a courier.
    Orders per courier
    Orders created in the last 60 minutes divided by couriers online now in that zone.
    Zone status
    Short: 3 or more orders waiting, or 2.6 or more orders per courier. Surplus: under 45% of online couriers busy.
    Idle courier
    Online, no order for 12 minutes or more.
    Reliability flag
    Last 7 days: rejects 30% or more of offers (25+ offers), or cancels 6% or more of orders (15+ orders).
